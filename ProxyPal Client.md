## 🌐 The Ultimate Proxy Management Desktop App

**ProxyPal Client** is a sleek, lightning-fast desktop application designed to give you total control over your network routing and privacy. Whether you are a developer testing geo-restricted APIs, a marketer scraping localized data, or a privacy enthusiast looking to secure your connection, ProxyPal makes managing proxies effortless.

Tired of manually entering IP addresses and ports into your browser settings? ProxyPal acts as a central hub. Add thousands of proxies, test their latency and speed with a single click, and instantly route your system traffic through the fastest nodes. It supports HTTP, HTTPS, SOCKS4, and SOCKS5, seamlessly integrating with any setup without requiring complex CLI commands.

> 💡 **Why use ProxyPal?** Because your IP address is your digital identity. Mask it, rotate it, and control it globally. With a beautiful native interface, ProxyPal turns a tedious technical task into a seamless one-click experience.

## 🚀 Quick Start

> **Three steps. Under two minutes. No tech skills required.**

### 1️⃣ Download the installer
Grab the latest `.exe` (Windows) or `.dmg` (macOS) from the button at the top of this README.

### 2️⃣ Add your proxies
Paste your proxy list into the "Import" tab. The app will instantly parse and categorize them.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Does it come with free proxies?</b></summary>

No. ProxyPal is a **client management tool**. You must supply your own proxy lists, which you can get from commercial proxy providers, or find free public lists online (though free proxies are usually slow and unsafe).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is my data safe?</b></summary>

Absolutely. All your proxy lists and settings are stored locally on your machine in an encrypted SQLite database. We do not have servers, and we do not track your browsing history.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use this for web scraping?</b></summary>

Yes! ProxyPal is built with automation in mind. The "Auto-Rotate" feature allows you to cycle through proxies at custom intervals, making it perfect for scraping tools like Python's `requests` or `Scrapy`.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it support SOCKS5 authentication?</b></summary>

Yes. You can add username and password authentication to any HTTP, HTTPS, or SOCKS5 proxy in the "Add Proxy" menu.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug?</b></summary>

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include your OS version, the app version, and a screenshot of the error log.
</details>
